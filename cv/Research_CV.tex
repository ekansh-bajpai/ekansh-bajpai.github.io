\section{Research experience}
\entry{Master's thesis research}{completed Oct 2026}{Automatic Control Group (RAT), Paderborn University}{%
\begin{itemize}
\item Core: replaced the CNN patch selector of the learned event-based visual odometry pipeline DEVO with spiking
      neural networks trained with surrogate gradients; compared two temporal encodings (replayed voxel grid, time-surface
      frames) against CNN baselines in 150 visual-odometry runs.
\item On MVSEC, the spiking selector achieved about $2\times$ lower absolute trajectory error and $10\times$ smaller spread
      than the CNN baselines; on held-out TartanEvents sequences both viable spiking variants beat both CNNs.
\item Showed with a same-data CNN control that the remaining gap on fast drone-racing motion (UZH-FPV) comes from the
      training data, not from spiking computation.
\item Generated and publicly released \textbf{TartanEvents}, a 1.3\,TB simulated event-camera training dataset.
\item Extension: built \textbf{SuperEventSLAM}, the first graph-based SLAM back-end for DEVO (keyframes, SuperEvent
      features, three-stage loop verification, g2o pose-graph optimization). The pipeline runs end to end at 36.7 FPS;
      loop verification is not yet reliable, so drift reduction is future work.
\end{itemize}}
\entry{Student assistant, humanoid robot learning}{Sep 2026 -- present}{Stratosfare -- Fraunhofer IEM, Paderborn}{%
\begin{itemize}
\item Implemented NVIDIA's GR00T end-to-end workflow on a real Unitree G1: teleoperated demonstrations, LeRobot dataset
      export, fine-tuning of the GR00T N1.7 vision-language-action model, and deployment via NVIDIA Jetson AGX Thor.
\item Collecting whole-body VLA training data with NVLabs GR00T-WholeBodyControl (GEAR-SONIC, PICO VR teleoperation).
\item Demonstrated the trained G1 picking an object and placing it in a container at a 3-day workshop and guided
      attendees through teleoperation.
\end{itemize}}
